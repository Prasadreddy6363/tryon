--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  🛠️ TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ISSUE: "White parts of the clothing are being removed"

SOLUTION:
  The threshold is too aggressive. In app.py, change:
  
  def remove_white_background(img, threshold=250):  # Increased from 240
  
  Higher threshold = removes only very bright whites
  Lower threshold = removes more shades of white

ISSUE: "Background still visible"

SOLUTION:
  Background might not be pure white. Try:
  • Pre-process image to make background pure white
  • Or lower threshold to 230 for more aggressive removal

ISSUE: "Edges look rough"

SOLUTION:
  • Use higher quality source images
  • Ensure clean, sharp edges on original
  • Or apply smoothing in image editor first
