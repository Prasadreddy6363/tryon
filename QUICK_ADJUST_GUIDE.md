# ⬆️⬇️ Quick Adjust Clothing Position Guide

## ✨ New Feature Added!

I've added **Quick Adjustment Buttons** to make it super easy to position clothing perfectly!

---

## 🎮 New Control Panel

### Location:
**Overlay Settings** section → **"⬆️⬇️ Quick Adjust Position"** panel

### 6 Quick Buttons:

#### **Position Controls:**
1. **⬆️ Move Up** - Moves clothing upward by 10 pixels
2. **⬇️ Move Down** - Moves clothing downward by 10 pixels  
3. **⬅️ Move Left** - Moves clothing left by 10 pixels
4. **➡️ Move Right** - Moves clothing right by 10 pixels

#### **Size Controls:**
5. **🔼 Larger** - Increases clothing size by 5%
6. **🔽 Smaller** - Decreases clothing size by 5%

---

## 🚀 How to Use:

### **Quick Positioning:**

```
1. Start Camera
2. Select Clothing
3. See clothing on your body
4. Click adjustment buttons to fine-tune:
   - Too low? Click "⬆️ Move Up" (repeat as needed)
   - Too high? Click "⬇️ Move Down"
   - Off to side? Click "⬅️ Move Left" or "➡️ Move Right"
   - Too small? Click "🔼 Larger"
   - Too big? Click "🔽 Smaller"
```

### **Each Click:**
- **Position buttons**: Move by 10 pixels
- **Size buttons**: Change by 5%
